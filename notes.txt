🧠 QUICK DEBUG CHECKLIST (USE DAILY)

Whenever compilation fails:

1️⃣ pwd → where am I?
2️⃣ ls → is the file here?
3️⃣ cd → go to correct folder
4️⃣ compile again

90% of terminal errors are solved here.
